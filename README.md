# GO NINJA GO 

GO NINJA GO is a small 2D platformer game me and my friend made

You play as a ninja and have to go  through different levels by jumping across platforms, collecting items, and fighting enemies. 
The goal is to reach the end of all three levels while getting the highest score possible.

## About the Game

we wanted to make a simple platformer where the player can move around freely and explore different stages.

The game has a mountain background and animated ninja character and enemies and and different platforms to jump on.

Each level has a different layout, so you have to figure out how to reach the end without losing all your health.

## Controls


 A  -  Move left 
 
 D - Move right 
 
 W  - Jump 
 
 S -  Crouch
 
 K  - Attack 
 
 Enter - Start game
 
 Esc - Restart 
 

The game can be played using just your keyboard.

## How to Play

1. Press Enter to start the game.
2. Use A and D to move your ninja.
3. Press W to jump over gaps and reach higher platforms.
4. Press K to attack enemies and break certain blocks.
5. Collect items along the way to increase your score.
6. Reach the end of the level to unlock the next stage.
7. Complete all three levels to finish the adventure.

Try to collect as many points as possible before reaching the end!
